About CardioRisk AI

Data Preparation

Patient cardiovascular information is processed before prediction. Relevant health features such as age, blood pressure, cholesterol, glucose, lifestyle information and BMI are used to prepare data for cardiovascular risk assessment.

XGBoost Risk Prediction

The XGBoost machine learning model analyzes patient health information to estimate cardiovascular risk. The prediction is displayed as a risk probability together with a Low, Moderate or High risk category.

ECG CNN Analysis

Uploaded ECG signals can be analyzed using a Convolutional Neural Network trained on ECG data. The CNN provides an AI-based classification of the ECG signal as Normal or Abnormal along with prediction probabilities.

AI Explainability with SHAP

SHAP explainability is used to help interpret the XGBoost model's prediction. It identifies the factors that had the strongest influence on the cardiovascular risk estimate and indicates whether each factor increased or decreased the model's predicted risk.

Security & Authentication

The application uses authentication to protect patient accounts and private assessment data. Authorized users can access their predictions, personalized guidance and assessment history.

Assessment History

Previous cardiovascular assessments are stored for authenticated users. This allows patients to review earlier risk predictions and monitor their cardiovascular assessment results over time.

Medical Disclaimer: CardioRisk AI is an educational and decision-support system. The predictions and AI analysis provided by this application are not a medical diagnosis and should not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
